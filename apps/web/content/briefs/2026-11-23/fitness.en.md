# What fitness videos actually get watched this season

Covers November 9 to 21, 2026. A quiet week for fitness-specific platform news, so this edition leans on fresh data and post ideas.

## This week

- **New data on 11,435 US fitness TikToks.** Virlo's study of fitness videos posted from August 18 to November 10, 2026 puts the typical video at about 13K views. Workout demos are the most common format in the top 10%, and silent aesthetic clips, comedy bits and challenges show up more often in the top 10% than in the niche overall. Motivational, tutorial and transformation videos do worse than their share would suggest. Videos posted 3 to 5 pm Eastern got about 18% more views, Saturday was the best day and Monday the weakest. Do this: post one workout demo and one comedy bit this week, both on Saturday afternoon. [Virlo, data through Nov 10](https://virlo.ai/data/fitness-influencers)
- **The trends people are searching for right now.** New Engen's fitness trends guide (updated November 12) lists "winter arc" (the October to year-end push, mostly bulking), lifting heavy, mobility and longevity, whole-food eating for protein and fiber, and social fitness like run clubs and Hyrox. It also notes that Dictionary.com named "maxxing" its 2026 word of the year, and that some of that content has pushed unsafe practices. Do this: make one post that ties your method to winter arc, and keep it safe and realistic. [New Engen, Nov 12](https://newengen.com/insights/social-media-fitness-trends/)
- **Threads search can now find recent questions.** On November 10, Threads added filters for date, post type, topic and profile. Why it matters: people ask training and nutrition questions there every day, and a good answer from you reaches people who have never seen your account. Do this: search "how many sets" or "protein" filtered to the last few days and answer three people properly. [Social Media Today, Nov 10](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/threads-adds-advanced-search-filters/874339/)

## 5 post ideas

- **TikTok or Reel, silent workout demo:** one exercise, three angles, no talking, form cues as on-screen text. Hook (on screen): "Most people cut this rep short."
- **Comedy bit, the gym at 6 pm in December:** act out the January crowd arriving early. Hook: "Every gym the week before New Year."
- **Carousel, winter arc week one:** a realistic 4 day plan with sets, reps and rest, for someone who has 45 minutes. Hook: "A winter arc you can actually keep."
- **Reel, a mobility snack:** a 60 second routine for people who sit all day, filmed in one take. Hook: "Do this before your next meeting."
- **Threads post, myth check:** one popular "maxxing" claim and what the evidence actually says. Hook: "This trend is everywhere. Here is what it does to your body."

## One thing to try this week

Move your two main posts to Saturday between 3 and 5 pm Eastern (or your audience's equivalent afternoon), and make one of them a silent demo. After a week, check reach to non-followers against your usual weekday posts.
