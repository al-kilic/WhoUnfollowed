# Fix your Stories, protect your videos, and get found on Threads

Covers November 9 to 21, 2026.

## This week

- **You can now edit a Story after posting it.** Since November 18, Instagram lets you change text, music, stickers and drawings for 15 minutes after you share a Story, so a typo no longer means deleting it and losing the views it already had. Why it matters: the first minutes are when your Story gets seen most, and reposting used to reset that. Do this: post, then check your Story once on your own phone right away and fix it in place (tap the three dots, then Edit story). [9to5Mac, Nov 18](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/18/instagram-finally-lets-users-edit-stories-after-publishing-but-theres-a-catch/)
- **Meta now flags reposts of your videos on Instagram.** Content protection, already on Facebook, came to Instagram on November 17. Eligible creators who enroll get alerts when someone reposts their original videos, and decide what to do about it. A small group is also testing detection of videos that copy your spoken words even when the visuals are changed with AI. Why it matters: reposts by bigger accounts can take the reach that should have brought people to you. Do this: check your Professional Dashboard for content protection and enroll if it shows up, because it only covers uploads from then on. [Social Media Today, Nov 17](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-expands-content-protection-to-instagram/874965/)
- **Threads search got real filters.** On November 10, Threads added search filters for date, post type, community or topic, and profile, plus an exact match option. Why it matters: you can now find recent questions in your topic and answer them while they are fresh, which is one of the simplest ways to get seen by people who don't follow you yet. Do this: search one phrase from your niche, filter to the last few days, and reply usefully to three posts. [Social Media Today, Nov 10](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/threads-adds-advanced-search-filters/874339/)
- **Meta's Creator Dashboard now works on the web.** Announced November 18: a redesigned web composer for posts and Reels with live previews, bulk video upload, a better content library and more performance insights, for Instagram and Facebook. Why it matters: planning a week of posts on a big screen is easier than on your phone. Do this: open the dashboard on a computer and preview your next three posts before they go out. [Social Media Today, Nov 18](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-brings-its-updated-creator-dashboard-to-the-web/875143/)
- **TikTok's music data keeps pointing to sound as a discovery tool.** TikTok's 2026 Music Impact Report (with Luminate, 4,000+ US users) says 45% of US users discover new music on the app, and 94% of songs that entered the Billboard Global 200 in 2025 had a viral TikTok moment first. Why it matters: sounds that are rising can carry a small account into feeds it would not reach on its own. Do this: pick one rising sound this week and use it early, not after it peaks. [Social Media Today, Nov 18](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-releases-2026-music-impact-report/875144/)

## 5 post ideas

- **Reel, "the mistake I fixed":** show one thing you got wrong in your work and how you fixed it. Hook: "I did this wrong for a year. Here is the fix."
- **Carousel, before and after:** one project or habit, first version on slide one, current version on the last slide, the steps in between. Hook: "Same idea, two years apart."
- **Threads post, a real question:** ask your niche one specific question you actually want answered, then reply to every answer. Hook: "Honest question for anyone who does this every week."
- **Story sequence, a poll:** two options for your next post and let viewers pick. Hook: "You choose what I post on Friday."
- **TikTok or Reel with a rising sound:** a 7 second clip that shows one result, no talking. Hook (on screen): "Wait for the last second."

## One thing to try this week

Spend 10 minutes a day on Threads search. Filter to the last day, find three posts in your topic from people you don't know, and leave a reply that adds something real. At the end of the week, upload a new export and check whether your new followers went up compared with last week.
