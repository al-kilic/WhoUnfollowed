# Amazon links in Reels, live shopping numbers, and beauty effects

Covers November 9 to 21, 2026.

## This week

- **Instagram added Amazon affiliate links to Reels and posts.** Since November 16, eligible creators can link their Instagram and Amazon Influencer accounts in the Professional Dashboard and tag Amazon products in Reels (up to 30) and feed posts (up to 20). Links show bottom left, open the Amazon app, and you see the commission rate per item. Why it matters: a product you mention can now be tapped right on the post, not hidden behind "link in bio". Do this: tag only what you actually used in your next routine Reel, and say so in the caption. [Social Media Today, Nov 16](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-adds-amazon-affiliate-links/874817/)
- **TikTok UK's first live shopping report.** Published the week of November 15: live shopping sales in the UK grew 55% year over year, beauty and fashion are top categories, and more than 60% of small sellers said one live session beat what other channels earned in a typical week. Why it matters: live is where beauty and fashion audiences buy, and the format favors honest demos over polished ads. Do this: plan one 20 minute live this week with a clear theme, like a 5 product party look. [Social Media Today, Nov 15](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-uk-publishes-live-shopping-report/874711/)
- **Build your own Stories effect from a photo.** Effect Builder (November 9) lets you describe an effect and upload a style reference photo, and Meta AI uses its color, lighting and texture. Approved effects appear in the Stories effects browser and link back to your profile. Why it matters: a beauty filter people like spreads on its own, with your name on it. Do this: make one subtle effect based on your signature look, and label it as AI when you post with it. [Social Media Today, Nov 9](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-launches-visual-effects-creator-for-edits/874205/)
- **TikTok is testing a paid shopping membership.** TikTok Shop Plus is being tested with select US users at $9.99 a month after a 30-day free trial, with more free shipping, exclusive discounts and instant refunds (reported November 10). Why it matters: TikTok built it to keep repeat buyers coming back, much like Amazon Prime, which matters if you sell or earn commission there. Do this: if you sell on TikTok Shop, check whether your products can carry the Plus perks. [Social Media Today, Nov 10](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-tests-shopping-subscriptions/874337/)

## 5 post ideas

- **Reel, the 5 product party look:** full face with only five products, each tagged. Hook: "Five products. One look. Under ten minutes."
- **Carousel, what I would not buy again:** three products you stopped using and why. Hook: "I spent money on these so you don't have to."
- **Live, get ready with me for a holiday party:** take questions while you work, name every product. Hook (title): "Holiday party look, ask me anything."
- **Reel, one outfit three ways:** one base piece styled for work, dinner and a weekend. Hook: "One coat, three days."
- **Threads post, ask for help:** post a real dilemma (which shade, which cut) and let people vote in replies. Hook: "Settle this for me before Friday."

## One thing to try this week

Run one short live with a single theme and tell people in your Stories a day ahead with a countdown sticker. Afterwards, compare new followers from that day with a normal posting day.
