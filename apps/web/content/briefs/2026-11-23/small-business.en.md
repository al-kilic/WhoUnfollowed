# Black Friday week: live selling, local search and fixable Stories

Covers November 9 to 21, 2026. Black Friday is November 27 and Small Business Saturday (in the US) is November 28.

## This week

- **TikTok UK's live shopping report has good news for small sellers.** Published the week of November 15: UK live shopping sales grew 55% year over year, more than 60% of small businesses said one live session earned more than other channels in a typical week, and about a third of live viewers later bought a product in a store. Why it matters: a live session can work for a shop with a counter and a phone, not just big brands. Do this: schedule one live during Black Friday week showing your best sellers up close. [Social Media Today, Nov 15](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-uk-publishes-live-shopping-report/874711/)
- **You can fix a Story for 15 minutes after posting.** Since November 18, Instagram lets you edit text, music, stickers and drawings on a Story you already shared, without deleting it. Why it matters: a wrong price, date or opening time used to mean reposting and losing views. Do this: post your opening hours and deals for the holiday week as Stories, and check each one right after posting. [9to5Mac, Nov 18](https://9to5mac.com/2026/11/18/instagram-finally-lets-users-edit-stories-after-publishing-but-theres-a-catch/)
- **Threads search can find local questions.** On November 10, Threads added filters for date, post type, topic and profile, plus exact match search. Why it matters: people ask for local recommendations every day, and a helpful reply puts your shop in front of people nearby who don't know you yet. Do this: search your city plus your product type, filter to the last week, and answer honestly (even when the best answer is not you). [Social Media Today, Nov 10](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/threads-adds-advanced-search-filters/874339/)
- **Brazil: Meta launched Achados, a local shopping app.** On November 19, Meta launched Achados in Brazil (Android only for now). It pulls Marketplace listings, Facebook Group posts and items sellers shared from Instagram into one feed of what people nearby are selling, with price comparison and links to seller profiles. Why it matters: if you sell in Brazil, items you share from Instagram can show up for people shopping nearby. Do this: make sure your product posts show a clear price and your city. [Social Media Today, Nov 19](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-launches-shopping-platform-in-brazil/875290/)

## 5 post ideas

- **Reel, behind the counter on Black Friday morning:** opening up, the first customer, the busiest shelf. Hook: "6 am at a small shop on the busiest day of the year."
- **Carousel, the gift guide by budget:** three price points, two products each, prices on every slide. Hook: "Gifts under 20, under 50 and under 100. All local."
- **Stories, Small Business Saturday countdown:** one sticker a day with what is coming. Hook: "Saturday is ours. Here is what we have planned."
- **Live, ask us anything about a product:** one product, how it is made or chosen, questions from viewers. Hook (title): "Why this is our best seller."
- **Threads post, an honest numbers post:** what Black Friday means for a small shop (margins, staff, stock), with no guilt trip. Hook: "What a discount actually costs a shop our size."

## One thing to try this week

Run one live during Black Friday week, mention it in Stories the day before with a countdown sticker, and post a short clip from it as a Reel the next day. Then upload a new export and see how many new followers came that week compared with the one before.
