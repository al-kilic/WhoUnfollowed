# Better edits, protected videos, and your look as a Stories effect

Covers November 9 to 21, 2026.

## This week

- **Edits added speed curves and automatic audio balancing.** On November 12, Meta's Edits app got speed curves (presets or your own curve to speed up and slow down inside one clip), automatic audio levels across clips, topic controls for the Inspiration tab, and control over how long text stays on screen. Why it matters: speed ramps used to need a desktop editor, and uneven audio is one of the fastest ways to lose a viewer. Do this: recut one older clip with a speed ramp on the key moment and repost it as a new Reel. [Social Media Today, Nov 12](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/metas-edits-app-gets-speed-curves-and-auto-audio-balancing/874586/)
- **Instagram now flags reposts of your original videos.** Content protection reached Instagram on November 17. Enrolled creators get alerts when their original videos are reposted on Instagram or Facebook, and a small test also catches videos that copy your spoken words with AI-changed visuals. Why it matters: shooters lose a lot of reach to repost accounts that crop out the credit. Do this: enroll if your Professional Dashboard offers it, before your next upload, since it only covers new uploads. [Social Media Today, Nov 17](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-expands-content-protection-to-instagram/874965/)
- **You can turn your color look into a Stories effect.** Effect Builder (November 9) is a web tool in Edits where you describe an effect, upload a photo as a style reference, and Meta AI uses its color, lighting and texture. Effects are reviewed (usually under 24 hours), show up in the Stories effects browser, and include a link back to your profile. Why it matters: every person who uses your effect sees where it came from, which is free discovery. Do this: build one effect from your signature grade and share it in your Stories. [Social Media Today, Nov 9](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-launches-visual-effects-creator-for-edits/874205/)
- **The Creator Dashboard is on the web, with bulk video upload.** Announced November 18: a web composer for posts and Reels with full-screen live previews, bulk upload and a better content library. Why it matters: you can check crops and covers on a big screen before they go live, instead of finding out on your phone. Do this: upload next week's Reels in one sitting and preview each cover on the grid. [Social Media Today, Nov 18](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/meta-brings-its-updated-creator-dashboard-to-the-web/875143/)

## 5 post ideas

- **Reel, one shot three speeds:** the same clip at normal speed, then with a speed ramp, then the final edit. Hook: "Same footage. The speed curve does the work."
- **Carousel, the grade breakdown:** slide one the final photo, then raw, then each step of the edit. Hook: "From flat file to final in four moves."
- **Stories, try my effect:** share the effect you built and repost the best photos people make with it (with their permission). Hook: "I made my edit into an effect. Show me yours."
- **Reel, the light at 4 pm:** a 10 second timelapse of light changing on one subject. Hook: "Why I only shoot this spot at one time of day."
- **Threads post, kit question:** ask which one lens people would keep if they had to sell the rest, and answer every reply. Hook: "You can keep one lens. Which one?"

## One thing to try this week

Post the same Reel twice in two edits a few days apart: one straight cut, one with a speed ramp on the key moment. Keep the caption and sound the same. Compare reach to non-followers in Insights after 48 hours each, and keep the style that reached more new people.
